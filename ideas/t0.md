# t0 — "make me money" as a frozen idea (2026-09-10)

Status: under test this turn as the e2e chain prompt. Append-only notes only.

## Problem

Vague revenue prompts fail two ways: agents hallucinate revenue plans with
fabricated numbers, or they stall asking clarifying questions forever. The
chain must turn "make me money" into measured triage + executed $0 steps +
exactly-specified H/M remainder — with zero invented dollars.

## Mechanism

Decompose the prompt against the estate: enumerate revenue surfaces, probe
each for liveness at $0 cost (402 quotes, local funnels, queue states), rank
by evidence (live + priced + receipt-path beats claimed), execute every
reversible $0 step, and queue the human-owned remainder (traffic, pilots,
spend approvals) with exact amounts and demos. Money-making that needs the
world (buyers, traffic) routes to H by construction; the agent prepares.

## Falsifiable predictions

- [ ] Triage lists ≥3 estate surfaces each with a live-status probe output.
- [ ] Total spend this run equals $0 (no grant activated, no payment sent).
- [ ] Every claimed figure traces to a probe output or receipt (audit passes).
- [ ] H/M remainder carries exact amounts + demos (no open-ended asks).

## Non-goals

Not a revenue event (no sale is closed this run). Not traffic generation.
Not touching live wallets, keys, or paid endpoints.

## Verification plan

Re-run the probes (curl 402 quote, hplane funnel, loop check); grep the
triage for `$` figures and trace each to a probe; confirm spend ledgers
empty; receipts verify.
