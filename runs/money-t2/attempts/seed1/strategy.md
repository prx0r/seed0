# Strategy: Same-day mobile pre-listing inspection + minor-repair wedge (Austin suburbs)

Opportunity: Same-day mobile pre-listing inspection + minor-repair wedge for suburban single-family homes in Austin, TX — a 90-minute physical validation visit ($189–$249) that photographs, tests, and fixes small defects before listing.

## Bottleneck mechanism

Abundance: AI staging, virtual listings, and cheap flip content have made every listing look perfect online, inducing extra buyer/seller demand for physical validation.
Scarcity: the supply of trusted, licensed humans who will physically show up, climb, test outlets, run water, and attest to condition responds slowly (licensing, driving, liability, scheduling).
The bottleneck is the physical validation slot: demand for proof grows faster than supply of provers, so the inspection-plus-small-fix visit captures the margin. This is the scarcity-thesis bottleneck in one sentence.

## Crowdedness read

The consensus trade is crowded software: AI appraisals, national inspection marketplaces, and virtual-tour startups chasing venture scale. That field is crowded with capital and code.
This wedge is not the consensus trade because it is unsexy, local, and operationally heavy: same-day driving, ladders, caulk guns, and liability waivers do not scale in a pitch deck, so few entrants persist. Incumbent inspectors book 3–7 days out and refuse minor repairs (conflict-of-interest posture), leaving the urgent pre-listing slot uncrowded and underserved.

## Falsifiable prediction

Prediction 1 (falsifiable): within 60 days of launch, from 100 inbound seller leads we will complete >=30 paid visits at an average price >=$189 with booking-to-paid conversion >=30%; we will prove it wrong (kill the thesis) if we complete <15 paid visits or average price <$149 or conversion <15%.
