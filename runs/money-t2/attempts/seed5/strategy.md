# Strategy: Third-party load-bank testing for AI data-center backup power (Northern Virginia)

Opportunity: independent load-bank testing + commissioning reports for backup generators at new AI data centers.

## Bottleneck mechanism
Abundance of AI compute announcements and data-center builds induces demand for physical validation faster than supply responds. Every new megawatt of claimed AI capacity must prove it can ride through a grid outage: generators, fuel, switchgear, and commissioning paperwork. The bottleneck is certified test capacity — load banks, licensed technicians, and utility-witnessed test windows. Demand for proof grows with each build announcement; supply of test crews and witness slots responds slowly (equipment lead times, licensing, utility scheduling).

## Crowdedness read
The crowded trade is selling picks-and-shovels to data centers (GPUs, racks, generic construction, chatbot agencies). Few small operators chase the unglamorous compliance tail: on-site load-bank testing and stamped commissioning reports. It is not the consensus trade because it is physical, local, scheduled work — not scalable software — so big capital and indie hackers alike ignore it. That is why the wedge is open.

## Falsifiable prediction
ONE falsifiable prediction: within 6 months of starting outreach (by 2026-03-10), we sign >= 3 paid test contracts at >= $8,000 each; otherwise the thesis is wrong. What would prove it wrong: fewer than 3 contracts, or median price below $8,000, or sales cycle exceeding 6 months from first contact to signed PO.
